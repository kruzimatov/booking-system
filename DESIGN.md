# Design system

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Direction

Booking uses a warm editorial interface for clients making a quick appointment decision on a phone. The visual language is human and local: paper-like surfaces, a clay accent, confident serif headings, and compact controls that keep the next action obvious.

## Foundations

- Canvas: warm off-white `#fcfaf7`, with restrained tonal depth rather than heavy shadows.
- Ink: deep brown `#2f2824`; secondary text stays muted but readable.
- Accent: clay `#bd5c39` for primary actions, selected states, and identity.
- Structure: thin warm borders, generous outer spacing, and asymmetrical soft corners on selectable options.
- Type: system sans-serif for controls and body copy; Georgia for headings to add editorial character without adding a dependency.

## Interaction

- Primary actions use the clay accent and remain full-width where the task is linear.
- Selected booking choices combine a tinted surface, stronger border, and subtle lift so state is visible without relying on color alone.
- Navigation uses a translucent header on the warm canvas and remains compact on mobile.
- Motion is limited to page entry and small affordance lifts, with reduced-motion support.

## Responsive intent

The booking flow is designed mobile-first at 375px: one decision column, readable touch targets, no horizontal scrolling, and a clear stepper. Wider screens add breathing room without turning the flow into a dashboard.
